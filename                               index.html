Who We Are

Malawi Predictive Intelligence (MPI) is a data science and artificial intelligence company specializing in predictive analytics, forecasting, and decision intelligence. We help governments, NGOs, and businesses anticipate risks, optimize resources, and make proactive decisions using mathematics, statistics, and computer science.

Crop yield forecasting and climate risk prediction tailored to Malawi’s local conditions.
